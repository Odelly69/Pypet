# Pypet Trophy Cabinet Design

Status: LOCKED

## Purpose
Trophies are physical world objects representing meaningful accomplishments. They are not merely achievement icons.

## Cabinet
- The player's home contains a physical trophy cabinet.
- Earned trophies become persistent display objects.
- The player can inspect, arrange, and expand the display.
- Trophy records retain accomplishment title, description, date, and optional associated active/past pet.
- Past-pet references never expose hidden lineage or genetic recipes.

## Design language
Every trophy is an original Pypet design inspired by broad real-world award traditions such as cups, shields, plaques, medals, statuettes, columns, and laurel forms. Designs must not copy a specific real trophy.

Each trophy should have:
- Distinct silhouette tied to the accomplishment.
- Ornate/fancy presentation appropriate to its importance.
- Pedestal or display base.
- Engraved title or accomplishment plate where appropriate.
- Materials/finishes appropriate to rarity.
- Idle display animation that remains calm and epilepsy-conscious.
- Optional inspection animation when selected.

- First Python Program: coding-inspired achievement cup.
- Python Mastery: tall multi-tier programmer's cup.
- Debugger: ornate debugging shield with an original bug motif.
- Problem Solver: sculptural puzzle/logic trophy.
- Scholar: open-book achievement cup.
- First Major Project: builder/engineering trophy.
- Garden Master: botanical cup.
- Explorer: compass/globe trophy.
- Evolution Milestone: abstract evolution spiral trophy.
- Pet Care: caregiver trophy featuring an original pet sculpture.
- Persistence: perseverance column trophy.
- Career Readiness: professional achievement cup.
- Academy Completion: grand academy trophy.
- World Builder: world-builder trophy.
- Playground Architect: playground-building trophy.

## Interaction
The cabinet is part of the world. The player can walk to it, open/inspect it, and rearrange displays. Trophies should be visible during normal home exploration. Other pets may pass through the room, but trophy display objects remain persistent.

## Safety
No strobing, rapid flashing, screen shaking, or reward-haptic effects. Reflective materials and animations must remain calm and configurable under accessibility settings.

## Economy
Trophies themselves are accomplishment records and are not sold as substitutes for achievements. Decorative cabinet upgrades may use the established progression/earned/purchase/rewarded-ad economy where appropriate.
