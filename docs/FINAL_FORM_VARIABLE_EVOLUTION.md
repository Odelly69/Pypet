# Variable Final-Form Evolution

The pet's final form is NOT permanent and is NOT predetermined.

## Core rule
The player only sees and knows the pet's CURRENT visible form/state. The eventual final form is a hidden, continuously evaluated outcome that depends on the pet's accumulated gameplay and development history.

## Development inputs
Final-form selection may consider the full game history, including:
- School attendance and lesson completion
- Python practice, debugging, challenges and mastery
- Pet care: hunger, hydration, hygiene, health, happiness and rest
- Play and interaction
- Exploration and World activities
- Daily engagement and streaks
- Streak-freeze use
- Trophies and milestones
- Projects and applied programming
- Balanced versus neglected development
- Lineage-specific traits and rare-variant conditions

The exact weighting should remain hidden from the player. The game should communicate current needs and broad positive behaviors, but should not reveal a formula that lets the player trivially optimize a final form.

## No death / no destructive punishment
Pets never die. Missed daily activity can reduce current stats and development momentum and can reduce the quality/probability of later outcomes, but it never deletes the pet, town, trophies, mastered lessons, or completed work.

## Dynamic outcome
The final form is a variable outcome. Continuing to play, learn, care for, and interact with the pet can change its development trajectory before the final evolution is reached. Therefore the player should not know the final form in advance.

## Player-facing design
Show only:
- Current pet form
- Current stats/needs
- Current lineage
- Current development indicators
- Available actions and their immediate/near-term effects

Do NOT show:
- Final-form name
- Final-form preview
- Exact final-form probability
- Exact hidden scoring formula
- A guaranteed final-form path

## Goal
Consistent, balanced participation should generally produce stronger opportunities for exceptional final forms, while varied play styles can produce different legitimate outcomes. Rare final forms remain possible for every lineage. The system rewards sustained engagement and learning without turning the game into a deterministic checklist.
