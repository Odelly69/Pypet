# Daily Play, Streaks & Pet Development

Daily engagement is encouraged, not punitive.

## Daily loop
School, hands-on Python practice, pet care, exploration, projects, and World activities all contribute to meaningful daily play. Practice and completed lessons count as pet learning exactly like other learning activities.

## Streaks
Streaks track consecutive active days and provide milestone rewards. They do not erase mastery, trophies, curriculum progress, pets, towns, or completed projects.

## Streak freezes
Players can earn and use streak freezes. A freeze protects the streak when a day is missed. The system should clearly show available freezes and never hide the consequence of an unused freeze.

## Consequences
Missing daily play affects only appropriate pet-development progression and streak-reward opportunities. It must not delete progress or punish the player with loss of already-earned achievements.

Pet-development consequences should be gradual and recoverable: reduced routine/learning momentum, missed daily-care opportunities, or delayed development bonuses. Returning to play restores momentum through normal care, learning, and positive interaction.

## Rewards
Use layered rewards: daily activity reward, streak milestone reward, school/lesson reward, module trophy, project reward, and major mastery reward. Rewards can include coins, cosmetics, World items, pet-care items, trophies, and rare lineage opportunities while keeping mastery as the educational gate.

## Psychology guardrails
Use streaks to create continuity and anticipation, not anxiety. No shame language, irreversible loss, forced timers, or fear-based notifications. A player should feel welcomed back after an absence.
