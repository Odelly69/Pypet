# Pypet Pet Evolution Design — Locked

## Core fantasy
Pypet is a world-heavy virtual pet game. The player raises pets inside their world. Python is learned as a game at the pet's school, and the user's learning progress contributes to the pet's development and world development.

## Eggs and lineage
- Every new pet journey starts with a unique, independently randomized egg.
- Egg appearance, pattern, identity seed, name seed and lineage are randomized.
- The player does not select the lineage before hatching.
- Example lineages include Fox, Unicorn, Dragon, Cat, Wolf, Bunny, Turtle, Deer, Bear, Frog, Bird, Fae and Nature/Spirit.
- Each lineage has its own evolution tree and its own rare-variant table.
- The same lineage can produce different forms and rare forms on different journeys.

## Evolution
Every evolution creates a new collectible pet form. Previous forms are preserved in the collection/encyclopedia rather than deleted.

There is no single starting pet, no single ending pet and no universal best species.

Evolution quality favors **all major activities equally**. The game should not let one activity, including Python, dominate the evolution result.

Equal-weight development categories:
1. Food/nutrition and hunger
2. Health/vitality
3. Care/grooming
4. Happiness
5. Play
6. School attendance
7. Python learning
8. Exploration
9. Routine/streak behavior

The balanced development score is derived from all nine categories. Higher balanced development unlocks stronger evolution tiers.

## Rare evolution
After an evolution tier is reached, a separate randomized rarity roll occurs within the pet's lineage. Each lineage has its own common, uncommon, rare and very rare/mythic possibilities.

Rare does not mean pay-to-win. Rare forms are primarily special in appearance, animation, sound, world interactions and collection value.

Paid purchases must never guarantee a particular evolution.

## School and Python
School is a physical world location. The pet goes to school, the player starts the Python learning game there, and the player's successful learning becomes the pet's education progress.

Python progress also develops the world. Curriculum milestones can unlock construction, interactive systems, automation, shops, labs and advanced projects.

The player should experience: walk to school → start lesson game → learn Python → pet learns with the player → world development unlocks → return to the world.

## World-first design
Primary world locations:
- Home
- School / Python Academy
- Hatchery
- Market / food shop
- Park / playground
- Pet Clinic
- Habitats
- Trophy Hall
- Wild/exploration areas
- Workshop / Lab
- Treasure Trove

Food can be earned through normal play, discovered through exploration, purchased with earned currency, or optionally obtained through rewarded advertising. Essential care and learning must remain playable without payment or ads.

## Multi-pet collection
Players should accumulate many pets over the full game. New eggs create new journeys. Evolved forms are added to the collection. Owned pets can be displayed in habitats/world locations and eventually swapped into the active companion slot.

The goal is discovery and raising many different pets, not reaching one universal final pet.

## Audio, animation and sensory safety
Use a cheerful, happy background tune appropriate to a family-friendly virtual-pet game, plus gentle pet/world sounds. Animations should feel alive but must avoid intentional flashing, strobing, rapid flicker, screen shake and reward haptics. Reduced-motion mode must be respected.

HD visual assets are the target for the final world presentation: world backgrounds, pets, eggs, buildings, food, UI illustrations and evolution moments should be designed as cohesive game art rather than placeholder utility controls.

## Monetization
Optional cosmetic/world purchases can be sold through Google Play. Rewarded ads may optionally provide coins/food. High-value purchase grants require authoritative trusted-backend purchase-token verification before production content is granted; local Android storage is not sufficient for production fraud protection.
