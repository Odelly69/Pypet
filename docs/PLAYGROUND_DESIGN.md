# Pypet Playground Design

The playground is a persistent living-world location. Equipment is acquired along the same balanced-development progression used elsewhere in Pypet. It is never a replacement for Python lessons; it provides physical/social play and world-building opportunities.

## Equipment progression

1. Picnic bench — social/rest landmark
2. Spring rider — rocking/balance play
3. Swing set — classic swinging
4. Slide — climbing/descending play
5. Seesaw — cooperative balance
6. Merry-go-round — multi-pet social spinning
7. Climbing frame — routes and exploration
8. Overhead bars — coordination/persistence
9. Play tower — connected climbing/bridge/slide system
10. Nature play area — logs, stepping stones and discovery

Unlocks use balanced development milestones, so nutrition/care, happiness/play, health, school/Python learning, exploration and routines all contribute rather than one activity dominating.

## Multi-pet behavior

Past pets remain persistent residents after they grow up and earn the next egg. Their ordinary needs/actions must be scoped to the pet currently being cared for; caring for Pet A must not refill Pet B's hunger or otherwise alter Pet B's personal stats.

At the world level, all owned/past pets can coexist and interact: play together, rest together, use playground equipment, discover objects, socialize, and generate memories. World unlocks are account/world progression, while pet needs and development remain per-pet.

The long-term goal is a large collection of living pets rather than replacing one pet with the next. A growth/evolution milestone grants a new egg opportunity while the grown pet remains in the world.

## Playground design principles

Equipment should be visually original Pypet designs inspired by real playground categories, not copied manufacturer products. The game uses calm animation and no flashing/strobe effects. Spinner and swing motion should be gentle and optionally reduced/disabled by the safety settings.

Real-world safety research informs the simulated layout. The 2025 CPSC Public Playground Safety Handbook identifies age-appropriate equipment categories including swings, slides, climbers, merry-go-rounds/spinners, ramps, spring rockers and overhead equipment. It also emphasizes protective surfacing, spacing, entrapment prevention, sharp-edge/pinch-point avoidance, anchoring and maintenance. See CPSC sources: https://www.cpsc.gov/Playground-Handbook and https://www.cpsc.gov/safety-education/safety-guides/playgrounds/public-playground-safety-checklist.

Pypet is a virtual game; these references are design/safety inspiration and are not a claim that the virtual playground is a certified real-world playground.
